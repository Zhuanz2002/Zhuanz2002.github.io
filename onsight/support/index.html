Support

SceneReader is an accessibility-focused visual and walking-navigation assistant for iPhone.

Safety: SceneReader is an assistive tool. It does not replace mobility training, a white cane, a guide dog, personal judgment, or attention to traffic and surrounding hazards.

Contact support

Email zhuangzhengyu20@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send private camera images or precise location unless support specifically requests them and you are comfortable sharing them.

Permissions

SceneReader needs camera access for visual assistance, location access for destination routing, and microphone plus speech-recognition access for spoken questions. You can review these permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

Destination or route does not appear

  • Confirm that Location Services and mobile data or Wi-Fi are available.
  • Search for a specific place and select one of the Google Places results.
  • Wait briefly for the walking route to load, then try again if the network request failed.

Visual assistance does not start

  • Confirm that camera permission is enabled.
  • Allow time for the on-device model to load.
  • Keep sufficient free storage and close memory-intensive apps if iOS reports memory pressure.

Manage saved destinations

Use Remove Saved Destination on the Navigation Setup screen. Deleting the app also deletes data in its sandbox unless it is restored from an Apple device backup.
