OnSight Beacon

Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

OnSight Beacon is local-first. It does not require an account, sell personal information, show advertising, or track users across apps and websites.

Information used by the app

  • Camera: Camera frames are processed on the device for visual assistance. In the release build, OnSight Beacon does not save those frames to its document storage.
  • Location: Precise location is used while the app is open to show the map, request a walking route, and calculate route guidance.
  • Destination searches: Search text and selected place details are sent to Google Places to return matching destinations.
  • Speech: Microphone audio is used when you choose Ask. Apple speech-recognition services may process the audio according to Apple's privacy terms. OnSight Beacon does not intentionally retain the recording.
  • Saved destinations: A destination name and coordinates are stored in the app's local sandbox when you save a destination. You can remove saved destinations in the app.

Google Maps Platform

OnSight Beacon uses Google Maps Platform, including Maps, Places, and Routes services. Requests may provide Google with location, destination-search information, device or app identifiers, product interaction, and diagnostic information needed to operate and improve those services. Google states that its iOS SDKs may collect precise and coarse location, search history, device identifiers, product interaction, crash data, performance data, and other technical data, depending on SDK use and configuration. This data is not used by OnSight Beacon for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Use of Google Maps features is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional cloud visual processing

The initial beta's visual model runs on the device. A future version may offer clearly labeled optional cloud modes. If a user chooses such a mode, a compressed camera image and the related prompt may be sent through an OnSight-controlled HTTPS service to an AI provider solely to produce the requested response. OnSight does not intentionally retain those images or prompts after servicing the request. This policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that capability is enabled for users.

Retention and control

OnSight Beacon retains saved destinations locally until you remove them or delete the app. Apple device backups may include app data according to the backup settings on your device. Map and speech service providers retain information according to their own policies. You may deny camera, location, microphone, or speech access in iOS Settings, although the related feature will not work.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS. No security measure is perfect, but the app is designed to minimize data collection and keep visual inference on the device in the initial beta.

Children

OnSight Beacon is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact us with a privacy concern.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will identify the current version.
