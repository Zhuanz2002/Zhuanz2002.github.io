SceneReader

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy describes the first SceneReader iPhone beta, which provides visual assistance, destination search and walking-route guidance.

Visual inference runs on your iPhone. This does not make every feature offline: Google map services and Apple speech recognition may process information off the device. No SceneReader account is required. We do not show advertising, sell personal information, or use your data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

Camera and spoken questions

Camera: SceneReader processes camera frames on the device for visual descriptions and assistance. In this beta, the app does not automatically save camera photos or videos to its document storage, and it does not upload camera frames for cloud visual inference.

Ask: When you activate Ask, microphone audio is provided to Apple’s speech-recognition framework to turn your question into text. Recognition may use Apple’s servers; this feature is not guaranteed to work entirely offline. SceneReader uses the recognized text to answer your question and does not create or retain audio-recording files in this beta.

Apple handles information processed by its services under its own terms and privacy practices. See Apple’s speech-recognition privacy information, including its section about apps using Speech Recognition.

Location, searches and Google services

SceneReader requests access to your location while you use the app. Precise location helps show the map and provide walking-route guidance. Destination search text is sent to Google Places to find matching places. Route requests to Google include your current location and destination coordinates.

The app uses Google Maps, Places and Routes. Depending on the service and SDK configuration, Google’s processing includes precise or coarse location, search history, device identifiers, product interactions, crash and performance information, and other technical data used for service functionality and analytics. Google SDK disclosures identify device identifiers and certain other technical data as linked to the user. Not requiring a SceneReader account does not mean all service data is anonymous.

Google service processing and retention are governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps terms. Our App Store privacy disclosures describe the data categories used by the app and its SDKs.

Saved destinations and retention

When you select a Google place as a destination, its name and coordinates are saved automatically in the app’s local storage. You can remove saved destinations in SceneReader. Deleting the app removes its local sandbox, but data in Apple device backups or separately exported copies may remain according to the settings and controls for those copies.

This beta does not keep app-created camera-photo or raw-audio recording files for later research use. Map and speech providers handle data under their own retention policies; SceneReader does not promise that information processed by those providers is immediately deleted or that we can delete information held independently by them.

Research and future features

The first pilot does not collect photos or recordings for our research or model-training program. Data processed by Google or Apple to provide existing features is separate from such a research program.

If a future version offers optional research-data sharing, we will first explain the data involved, purpose, storage access, retention, and withdrawal or deletion process, and obtain explicit opt-in consent before enabling that collection. Future cloud visual processing would also require a clear disclosure and a policy update before it is enabled. Neither feature is enabled in this beta.

Your controls and security

You can grant or revoke camera, location, microphone and speech-recognition permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission prevents or limits the related feature. You can remove saved destinations in the app and manage Apple backups separately.

SceneReader uses HTTPS for its Google web-service requests. We aim to minimize data use, but no security measure is perfect. Third-party service providers apply their own security and retention practices.

We will update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. An effective date will identify each published version.
